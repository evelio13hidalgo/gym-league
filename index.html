      How LP works
      • Each tier has 4 divisions (IV → I), 100 LP each.
      • Gain: minutes ÷ 4 × intensity × type. Higher RPE earns more; mobility earns less.
      • Hot streak: +5 LP when you've trained 3+ days in the last week.
      • Climbing gets harder — Diamond earns 60% of what Bronze does.
      • Only 2 sessions a day count (the second is worth half).
      • Decay: after 2 rest days you lose 5 LP, then 8, 11… per idle day. High tiers decay faster, and you can be demoted.
